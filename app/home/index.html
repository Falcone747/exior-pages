THE STRUCTURAL CHANGE

The rate of innovation changed. The way companies adapt did not.

Historically, an operating model could remain broadly valid for years. Today, the cost of intelligence, software, coordination, analysis and execution changes continuously. Every major capability shift changes what it is economically rational for a company to do itself, buy, automate, consolidate, remove or start selling.

01

Technology changes the cost curve.

Work that once required teams, handoffs and management layers can become software-led, agent-led or materially more efficient.

02

Value moves across old sector boundaries.

Your next competitor may be a platform, a software company, a financial player or a new entrant built on a radically lighter operating model.

03

Competitive advantage now expires faster.

A one-off transformation is not enough. The company needs a permanent capability for converting new innovation into economic advantage.
